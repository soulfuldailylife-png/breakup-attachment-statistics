## 2. Key Findings & Statistics

### A. Breakup Recovery Timelines
* **The 11-Week Recovery Curve:** Longitudinal research published in the *Journal of Positive Psychology* shows that approximately **71%** of individuals experiencing non-marital breakups reach subjective emotional equilibrium and measurable personal growth around **week 11**.
* **Cohabitation & Marital Separation:** Recovery from long-term cohabiting relationships requires an average of **17 to 18 months** to return to baseline psychological well-being (*Australian Institute of Family Studies*).

### B. Neuroimaging & Somatic Pain
* Functional MRI (fMRI) studies published in *PNAS* confirm that intense social rejection and romantic heartbreak activate the **secondary somatosensory cortex** and **dorsal anterior cingulate cortex**—the identical neural networks responsible for physical pain processing.

### C. Adult Attachment Distribution
* **Secure Attachment:** ~55% of the general adult population.
* **Dismissive-Avoidant:** ~25% (characterized by emotional deactivating strategies, withdrawal under intimacy pressure).
* **Anxious-Preoccupied:** ~20% (characterized by hyper-activating strategies, compulsive reassurance seeking).

### D. Modern Dating Fatigue
* **78.4%** of active dating app users report chronic emotional burnout or app fatigue (*Kinsey Institute*).
* **62%** of daters report having experienced ghosting in the past 12 months.
* **53%** of singles are transitioning toward "slow dating"—deliberately prioritizing fewer, values-based interactions over volume swiping.
